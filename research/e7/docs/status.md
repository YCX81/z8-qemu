# Evidence scope — 2026-10-22

This checkpoint publishes authored CPU components and dated research summaries.
The static resource identities in `resource-identities.json` cover nine recognized
PCNNP resources. Animal and vehicle CDNN/NeuPro containers are another serialized
representation; these counts do not establish eleven independent recognition
models or absence of other formats.

Resource744 is the bird network associated with the separately pinned E7-bird
reference. Resource700 is associated with body/face/head processing, 710/720 with
eye-related processing, 740/750 with animal/vehicle representations. The uses and
consumers of 754/760/770 are not fully established. Shape or a mode number alone
does not assign a semantic class.

## Fresh publication checks

- The initial research suite passed 48 tests with explicit private MAIN,
  calibration inventory and optional CPU Torch dependencies, including a bitwise
  comparison of the two resource744 heads with the pinned peer's procedural
  stripe fixture. This checks this CPU implementation on that finite fixture,
  not a new camera or accelerator run.
- Without private inputs or Torch, the same public suite passed with 33 explicit
  skips. Parser, synthetic channel, output-safety and CLI-help contracts ran.
- The new input/output adapter measured 100% statement/branch coverage over its
  31 statements and 12 branches. This is not coverage of all inference paths,
  firmware instructions, or the private vendor-backend pipelines.
- Source provenance, strict publication inventory and reachable Git history are
  checked separately from runtime results.

## Historical results retained as summaries

The dated model report covers 14 fixed COCO images, 43 PCNN input configurations
and 70 candidate forwards, with separately described animal/vehicle control and
postprocessing experiments. The peer report uses the same images and distinguishes
input resolutions, normalization and task definitions. Reports retain failures,
unresolved model semantics and the mismatch between torso/part boxes and whole
person ground truth. Their figures are not official mAP or general model rankings.

Those full benchmark, native instruction and vendor-backend runs were not rerun
for publication. Their raw photographs, tensors, weights, SDKs and session buffers
are absent. Their relative paths refer to the original private lab. The published
CPU subset does not include every native backend, parameter exporter or BA2
interpreter used by those historical experiments.

An original control/postprocess slice running around a candidate device model
does not independently validate the original NPU arithmetic. Camera ISP input,
actual ROI policy, complete model-specific consumers and physical AF behavior
remain separate acceptance gates. No camera was flashed or operated here.
